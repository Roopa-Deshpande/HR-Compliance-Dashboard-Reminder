Payment of Gratuity – Key Obligations

Gratuity Registration (Form A)
Within 30 days of applicability
Required when establishment has 10+ employees.
Notice of Change (Form B)
Within 30 days of any change
Report any change in name, address, or nature of business.
Employee Nomination (Form F)
Within 30 days of 1 year service
Employee submits nomination for gratuity benefits.
Gratuity Payment (Form I / L)
Within 30 days of eligibility
Employee applies via Form I; Employer responds via Form L.
Annual Return (Form R)
31st January each year
Details of gratuity payments made during previous year.
Gratuity on Death (Form J / K)
Within 30 days of claim
Nominee or legal heir files Form J / Form K.

Payment of Bonus – Key Obligations

Allocable Surplus (Form A)
Before payment of bonus
Calculate and record allocable surplus before paying bonus.
Set-on / Set-off (Form B)
Before payment of bonus
Determines surplus carried forward or deficit set off.
Bonus Register (Form C)
Within 8 months of FY end
Details bonus payments to eligible employees.
Annual Return (Form D)
30th December each year
Filed with labour department, summarising bonus payments.

Karnataka S&E Act – Quarterly Obligations

Employment Exchange Return (ER-1)
Quarterly – 30 days after quarter end
Applicable to establishments with 25+ employees under Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959.
CLRA Contractor Register Verification
Quarterly – internal review
Verify Register of Contractors (Form XII) and ensure all contractors are licensed (20+ workers threshold).
Minimum Wages – Quarterly Return
Quarterly – as per state notification
Submit wage returns to the Labour Department as required by Karnataka state rules.
Inter-State Migrant Workmen Return
Half-yearly / Quarterly
Return of interstate migrant workmen if 5+ such workers are employed. Form XIV to the Labour Commissioner.

Karnataka S&E Act – Additional Annual Obligations

S&E Annual Return (Form Q)
31st January every year
Annual return under Karnataka Shops & Commercial Establishments Act, 1961 – submission to Inspector of Shops.
Minimum Wages Annual Return (Form III)
31st January
Under Minimum Wages Act, 1948. Details of wage payments and working hours submitted online.
CLRA Annual Return
31st January / as notified
Principal Employer's Annual Return to registering officer under CLRA, 1970. Form XXIV (as per rules).
ESIC – Form 1A Annual Return
If any changes to address / bank / directors
Annual changes to employer registration details submitted to ESIC office.
Equal Remuneration – Form D
31st January
Annual return under Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 showing male/female employee details and wages paid.
Maternity Benefit – Annual Return
31st December
Return of maternity benefits paid to women employees in preceding year.
She-Box / POSH Return
31st January
Annual report of ICC (Internal Complaints Committee) under POSH Act, 2013. Submit online via She-Box portal.
Standing Orders – Form 5A
Once certified; review on change
Under Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act. 100+ employees. Submit to Certifying Officer.
Factory License Renewal (Elkhill / Terralife)
Before 31st December of expiry year
Factories Act, 1948. Renewal application to District Factory Inspector before expiry.
LWF – Form D Annual Return
15th January
Labour Welfare Fund annual return and contribution remittance under Karnataka LWF Act.
License & Registration Tracker
Auto-highlight rules: License cards turn amber when expiry is within 6 months or employee count reaches 80% of licensed limit. They turn red when expired or limit exceeded.

Karnataka S&E Act – Licensing Obligations

Shops & Establishment Registration
Before commencing business
Mandatory under Karnataka S&E Act, 1961. Renewal every 5 years. Display certificate at premises.
Contract Labour (CLRA) – Registration
Before engaging contract workers
Principal employer with 20+ contract workers must register. Contractor must obtain licence. Form I / Form IV.
Inter-State Migrant Workmen – Registration
Before engaging ISMW
Both principal employer and contractor registration required if 5+ migrant workers. Form I / Form V under ISMWA, 1979.
Factory Licence (Elkhill / Terralife)
Before commencing manufacturing
Under Factories Act, 1948. Renewed annually. Display at factory gate.
EPFO Registration
Within 30 days of reaching 20 employees
Mandatory PF registration under EPF & MP Act, 1952.
ESIC Registration
Within 15 days of reaching 10 employees
Mandatory ESI registration under ESIC Act, 1948. Maintain IP cards for all covered employees.
